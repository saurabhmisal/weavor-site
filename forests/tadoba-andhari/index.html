The landscape & its story

Get to know Tadoba-Andhari.

Water is a useful place to begin understanding Tadoba-Andhari. Lakes, seasonal pools, teak woodland and bamboo create a landscape that changes visibly through the year. Watch the smaller movements around the water as well as the larger mammals: a kingfisher hunting, deer approaching cautiously or birds gathering in the shade.

The reserve protects a wider web of life that includes tigers, leopards, sloth bears and wild dogs. Our naturalists connect the tracks and calls along your permitted route to the habitat around you. The choice of gate, season and stay shapes your experience here.

Planning your visit

Make room for the journey.

When to travel

Tadoba changes markedly between the cool season, hot summer and monsoon. Core and buffer access arrangements can differ, so we check the current season, entry gate and permit availability for your dates.

Getting there

Nagpur airport → the Khutwanda-side lodge area: around 2½ hours by road. Other gates and stays can require different transfers.

How long to stay

Consider 3–5 nights as a starting point, leaving time for several drives and rest between them. We adapt this to your interests and the rest of your India journey.

Transfer times are approximate. Your final route depends on traffic, the entry gate and accommodation; current access and permits are checked for your travel dates.

Questions about Tadoba-Andhari.

Which Tadoba gate is best?

Entry gates serve different zones and lodges. Rather than choosing a gate on reputation alone, we review your dates, permits and preferred accommodation.

Can I visit in summer?

Summer can be hot and dry. It can also be an interesting season for wildlife around water, but comfort and park conditions need careful consideration.
