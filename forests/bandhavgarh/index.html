Get to know Bandhavgarh

See the forest through local eyes.

The first thing you notice in Bandhavgarh is the changing shape of the forest. One minute the road runs beneath tall sal trees. The next, sunlight falls across a meadow and the whole landscape opens up. With a local naturalist alongside you, the small clues begin to make sense: a line of footprints, a sudden alarm call, the movement of a bird above the trees.

Bandhavgarh is famous for tigers, but its streams, grasslands and wooded hills also provide a home for deer, wild dogs, leopards and more than 250 recorded bird species. The ruins of an old hill fort add another layer to the place, although access to the fort itself is restricted.

A classic Central Indian forest for curious wildlife watchers and people who enjoy taking their time.

Our journeys are about learning to notice the forest, not chasing wildlife for a photograph. There's always more to discover, even when a tiger stays out of sight.

Understand the landscape

The forest around the fort

Bandhavgarh combines important big cat habitat with older human history. Its forest valleys, grasslands and rocky slopes support different prey animals, which in turn support predators. Your naturalist can explain these relationships without turning every drive into a race to find a tiger.

What to know before you go.

We'll help you plan around park regulations, permit availability and sensible travel connections. The right pace matters more than collecting as many drives as possible.

01

When to travel

Core safari zones typically operate from mid-October into June. Monsoon closures and weekly schedules vary, so exact dates and permits should be checked before you travel.

02

Getting there

Jabalpur is a common air gateway. Travellers also reach the area by rail via Umaria or Katni, followed by a road transfer. The practical route depends on your lodge and safari gate.

03

How long to stay

We suggest starting with 3 to 5 nights. We'll work out an itinerary that suits your pace, preferred accommodation and other places you hope to visit in India.

Safari permits, entry zones, vehicle arrangements, access restrictions and seasons are determined by the responsible forest authorities. We confirm current rules before you book.

Before you travel

Things you may be wondering.

Is Bandhavgarh a good choice for a first tiger safari?

It can be. Bandhavgarh has established safari routes and varied habitats. We suggest allowing several drives and appreciating the wider wildlife, since tiger sightings cannot be promised.

Which safari zone should I choose?

Tala, Magadhi and Khitauli are among the well-known core zones. The best choice depends on available permits, your lodging and current park guidance.

Can I visit Bandhavgarh Fort?

The historic fort is part of the landscape, but normal visitor access is restricted. Your naturalist can explain its history and significance from permitted routes.

How much time do I need?

Three to five nights is a useful starting point if you want multiple forest drives without rushing.

Will I definitely see a tiger?

No. Tigers and other animals are wild, and no sighting is guaranteed. We suggest allowing several drives and staying curious about everything the forest reveals.
