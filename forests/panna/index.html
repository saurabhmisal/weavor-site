The landscape & its story

Get to know Panna.

The Ken River gives Panna its character. Rocky slopes, dry woodland and river habitats bring together different kinds of wildlife, from vultures above the cliffs to birds along the water. Looking beyond the safari track helps you understand how animals use shade, cover and the river through changing conditions.

Panna also tells an important story of tiger restoration after the loss of its resident population. That recovery forms part of a wider conservation landscape, rather than a promise of a sighting. Nearby Khajuraho offers a natural cultural addition if you would like your India journey to include time beyond the forest.

Planning your visit

Make room for the journey.

When to travel

The forest department lists mid-October to mid-June as the best time to visit. Safari opening dates and river activities depend on current park schedules; confirm the exact dates and permits before travel.

Getting there

Khajuraho is the nearby gateway, about 32 km (20 miles) from Panna National Park. We confirm the driving time for your chosen gate and stay.

How long to stay

Consider 2–4 nights as a starting point, leaving time for several drives and rest between them. We adapt this to your interests and the rest of your India journey.

Transfer times are approximate. Your final route depends on traffic, the entry gate and accommodation; current access and permits are checked for your travel dates.

About this forest

Questions about Panna.

Why is Panna important for tiger conservation?

Tigers were reintroduced after the resident population was lost. The population recovery is an example of sustained conservation and monitoring.

Can I see crocodiles on a standard jeep safari?

Panna’s river ecosystem supports gharials and mugger crocodiles, but river sightings and access depend on permitted routes and conditions. They are not part of every jeep safari.

Can I combine Panna with Khajuraho?

Yes. Many travellers find the combination appealing because Panna offers a wildlife experience alongside Khajuraho’s historic temples.
